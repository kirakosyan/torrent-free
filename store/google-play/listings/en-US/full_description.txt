Torrent Client is a free, open-source BitTorrent app and torrent downloader for Android. Tap a magnet link in your browser or open a .torrent file, and the download starts. No ads, no account, no bundled offers.

Download and seed
• Open magnet links and .torrent files from your browser, file manager and other apps.
• See progress, download and upload speed, seeds, peers and time remaining.
• Start, pause, stop and remove transfers one at a time or all at once.
• Downloads keep running in the background with a progress notification.
• Transfers that were running resume automatically after a restart.

Stay in control
• Wi-Fi-only mode pauses transfers on mobile data and resumes them on Wi-Fi.
• Optional keep-awake setting for long transfers with the screen off.
• Global and per-torrent download and upload limits.
• Limit active downloads and seeds, with seeding ratio and time limits.
• Copy finished downloads to your phone's Downloads folder with one tap.

Privacy
No advertising or analytics SDKs. As with any BitTorrent app, trackers and peers can see your IP address; route supported traffic through your own SOCKS5 proxy if you prefer.

More
• Send finished audiobook downloads to LibroNest.
• System, light and dark themes, and 24 interface languages.

Android battery settings may limit background activity. Download and share only content you have the right to use.
