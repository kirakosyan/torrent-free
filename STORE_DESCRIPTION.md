# Microsoft Store description

## en-US

### Short description

Fast clean and free torrent downloader. No ads. No tracking. Ever.

### Full description

Torrent Free is a simple clean torrent downloader built for people who just want downloads to work. There are no ads. There are no subscriptions. There are no sign-ups. Add a magnet link or open a .torrent file and Torrent Free takes care of the rest.

Downloads can continue in the background so you can leave the app and come back to finished files. Speed limits help keep torrents from slowing down the rest of your connection. Progress notifications keep you informed without making you watch the app.

Torrent Free is free and open source. Files are saved directly on your device with no cloud middleman.

### Features

1. Add magnet links
2. Open .torrent files
3. Background downloads
4. Download speed limits
5. Upload speed limits
6. Progress notifications
7. Simple download queue
8. Save files directly to your device
9. No ads and no tracking
10. Free and open source
