SHORT DESCRIPTION

Fast, clean, and free torrent downloader. No ads. No tracking. Ever.


FULL DESCRIPTION

Torrent Free is a simple, clean torrent downloader that just works — no ads, no subscriptions, no sign-ups required.

Add any torrent using a magnet link or a .torrent file, and Torrent Free takes care of the rest. Downloads continue in the background so you can put your phone down and come back to finished files.


WHY TORRENT FREE?

🚀 Fast and lightweight — no bloat, no upsells, just downloads
🔒 100% private — nothing is tracked, logged, or shared
📴 Downloads in the background — even when the screen is off
⚡ Speed controls — set download and upload speed limits so torrents never slow down the rest of your connection
🔔 Progress notifications — stay informed without keeping the app open
🆓 Completely free and open source — no hidden costs, inspect the code yourself


HOW IT WORKS

1. Copy a magnet link or open a .torrent file
2. Torrent Free adds it to your download queue automatically
3. Your files are saved directly to your device — no cloud, no middleman


Torrent Free is open source and community driven. Contributions are welcome on GitHub.
